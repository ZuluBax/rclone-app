Privacy policy

This app is a personal installation of rclone used by its owner only.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, to copy them to and from the owner's own computers.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared, sold or sent to any third party.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
